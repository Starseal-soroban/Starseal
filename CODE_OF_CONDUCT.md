# Code of conduct

Starseal is a place to build software together. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, reviews or chat, is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and help newcomers find their way.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory remarks
- Personal attacks or sustained disruption of discussion
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Spam, including low-effort pull requests or repeated assignment requests

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, contact the maintainers through the contact details on the [Starseal-soroban](https://github.com/Starseal-soroban) organization page.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
